Phrasebook

Everything from your notes, corrected and written the way people say it in Spain. Tap the speaker to hear it.

Sounds in Spain
  • ce, ci, z = “th” as in think
  • j, ge, gi = throaty “h”
  • ll, y = “y”
  • ñ = “ny”
  • v = “b”
  • h is silent
  • rr is rolled
  • CAPITALS = stressed syllable

Reflexive verbs

Actions you do to yourself. The little word goes before the verb.

Is, are, there is

es / son
What something is, permanently. Es mi padre. Es divertido.
está / están
Where something is, or how it is right now. Está caliente. Está abierto.
hay
There is / there are. Hay mucho ruido.
este / esta / esto
This. Este for masculine nouns, esta for feminine, esto when you don't name the thing. Esto es importante.
probar / intentar
Both mean “try”. Probar is to taste or test something, intentar is to attempt to do it. Quiero probar la cerveza. Quiero intentar saltar.

Irregular past participles

These replace the usual -ado / -ido after he, has, ha… So it's he escrito, not he escribido.
